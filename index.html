NasehatKu

A collection of advice from Islamic preachers. Each entry holds the supporting verse or hadith (Arabic text, translation, and source), an explanation, its context, and what readers can put into practice. Anyone can read without an account; only registered preachers can write. Ships as an Android APK, plus a web version for iPhone.
