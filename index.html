NasehatKu

Advice from mubaligh, on Android and the web

A collection of advice from mubaligh. Each entry holds the dalil (Arabic text, translation, and source), an explanation, its context, and what readers can put into practice. Anyone can read without an account; only registered mubaligh can write. Ships as an Android APK, plus a web version for iPhone.
